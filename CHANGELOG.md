# Release notes

Version history in brief. Dates are 2026.

## 3.4.x (Aug 25 - Oct 6)
- **3.4.162** Data credits in the Help window.
- **3.4.161** Fixed slivers of buildings left at the far end of the X / Y cutaway.
- **3.4.159 - 3.4.160** Terrain: four distinct layer colours; the ground is exported as a few polyface meshes that CAD reads as one shaded surface.
- **3.4.149** Guided tour and a refreshed Help window.
- **3.4.144 - 3.4.148** Export image (PNG) and Export DXF (CAD): sections in elevation, footprints, streets and street names in Plan, 3D masses with real heights in Perspective, cutaway applied.
- **3.4.130** Poché is unlit, so lighting can no longer wash it out.
- **3.4.133** Terrain toggle: buildings, ground, streets, negative space and sections follow the real ground.
- **3.4.104** Shareable links: district, cutaways and layers are stored in the URL.
- **3.4.0** Draw a section box in Plan; elevations show only what is inside it.
- Plus many fixes to section, poché, camera and panel behaviour.

## 3.3 - 3.2 (Aug 17 - Aug 25)
- Position-dependent sections (3.3.0).
- All twelve Manhattan Community Districts, one by one from District 1 (3.2.x).

## 3.1 - 3.0 (Aug 12 - Aug 15)
- Per-neighborhood streets and permanent street-name labels (3.1.x).
- Manhattan context layer (3.0.15) and a major-streets overlay (3.0.26).

## 1.x - 2.x (Aug 10 onward)
- First versions: negative space carved from real building volumes, height cut, and the X / Z quadrant cutaway (1.4.0).

The full internal log is not published.
